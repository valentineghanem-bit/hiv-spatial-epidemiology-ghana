Do not sell this paper as district-level HIV determinant discovery. That version will not survive a careful Cambridge review.

The stronger paper is now clear: an ecological spatial epidemiology study showing how Ghana's DHS regional HIV estimates look when mapped onto district boundaries, with a frank audit of what public aggregate data can and cannot support. That honesty is the S+ move. It turns the main weakness into the paper's methodological value.
